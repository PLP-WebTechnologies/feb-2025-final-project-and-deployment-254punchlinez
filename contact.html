Frequently Asked Questions

How can I contribute an article to MindfulBlog?

We welcome guest contributions from writers who share our values and can provide valuable insights to our readers. Please send your pitch or completed article to submissions@mindfullblog.com with the subject line "Guest Post Submission".

Do you offer advertising or sponsorship opportunities?

Yes, we offer various advertising and sponsorship opportunities for brands that align with our values. Please contact us at partnerships@mindfullblog.com for more information.

How often do you publish new content?

We publish new articles three times a week, typically on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. Subscribe to our newsletter to get notified when new content is available.

I found an error in one of your articles. How can I report it?

We strive for accuracy in all our content. If you've found an error, please let us know by emailing corrections@mindfullblog.com with the article title and a description of the error.
